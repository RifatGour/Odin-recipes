Chocolate Chip Cookies

Description

Chocolate chip cookies are classic homemade cookies made with butter, sugar, flour, and chocolate chips. They have a soft, buttery center with lightly crisp edges.

These cookies are simple to prepare and are perfect as a snack or dessert with a glass of milk or a cup of tea.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Cream the butter, white sugar, and brown sugar together.
  3. Add the egg and vanilla extract and mix well.
  4. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture.
  6. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  7. Place spoonfuls of dough onto a baking sheet.
  8. Leave enough space between the cookies for them to spread.
  9. Bake for approximately 10 to 12 minutes.
  10. Allow the cookies to cool before serving.

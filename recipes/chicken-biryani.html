Chicken Biryani

Description

Chicken biryani is a fragrant rice dish made with basmati rice, marinated chicken, aromatic spices, and herbs. The ingredients are layered and cooked together so that the rice absorbs the flavors of the chicken and spices.

This flavorful dish can be served with raita, salad, or a simple side of fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • Fresh coriander
  • Fresh mint
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Whole spices
  • Salt
  • Water

Steps

  1. Wash the basmati rice thoroughly and soak it for about 20 minutes.
  2. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, spices, and salt.
  3. Slice the onion and cook it in oil until golden brown.
  4. Add the marinated chicken and cook until it is partially done.
  5. Add the tomatoes and cook until they become soft.
  6. Boil water in a large pot and season it with salt and whole spices.
  7. Add the soaked rice and cook until it is partially cooked.
  8. Drain the rice and layer it over the chicken.
  9. Add mint, coriander, and some fried onions on top.
  10. Cover the pot and cook on low heat until the rice and chicken are fully cooked.
  11. Gently mix the layers and serve hot.

Italian Mushroom Ravioli

Description

Italian mushroom ravioli is a comforting pasta dish filled with savory mushrooms, creamy ricotta, and Parmesan cheese. The ravioli is served with a simple butter and herb sauce.

This homemade-style recipe combines tender pasta with a rich, earthy filling and a light sauce, making it a delicious meal for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • Ravioli pasta sheets
  • 200 g mushrooms
  • 100 g ricotta cheese
  • 50 g Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tablespoon fresh parsley
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Water for boiling

Steps

  1. Clean and finely chop the mushrooms.
  2. Cook the mushrooms in a pan with a little butter until soft.
  3. Mix the cooked mushrooms with ricotta and Parmesan cheese.
  4. Season the filling with salt and black pepper.
  5. Place small portions of the filling onto the pasta sheets.
  6. Cover with another pasta sheet and seal the edges firmly.
  7. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  8. Cook the ravioli until they float to the surface.
  9. Melt butter in a pan and add minced garlic and parsley.
  10. Toss the cooked ravioli gently in the butter sauce.
  11. Serve immediately with additional Parmesan cheese.
